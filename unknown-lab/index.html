Sources, scope & interpretation

A defined analytical scheme

Based on Gilreath, Qualitative Analysis Using Semimicro Methods (1954), Part II, Chapters 12–21, with supplementary reaction references by James P. Birk, Chemistry LibreTexts. Group numbering belongs to this scheme.

The page contains 46 endpoint entries. It does not supply a fully executable SOP: volumes, concentrations, washes and equipment controls must come from your approved procedure. “Prepared fraction” is a prerequisite, not an automatic separation performed by this page.

Images are comparison aids

Each included photograph has a caption and source. Product photographs do not prove sample history, timing, phase, or selectivity. Where a matching image is unavailable, the page states that explicitly. No synthetic photo is presented as experimental evidence.

Historical fluoride glass etching is reference-only. Hazardous historical reagents require approved procedures and validated replacements. Do not identify fluoride by elimination.

How conclusions are worded

Positive means “supported under the stated conditions.” Negative means “not detected under these conditions.” Uncertain or unperformed tests do not exclude an ion. Redox preparation may prevent inference of the original oxidation state.
