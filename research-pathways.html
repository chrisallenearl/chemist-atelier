    Part 2 · Chemist Atelier

    How well does a candidate project fit?

    Think of one possible thesis while answering. This section scores project readiness and shows which sub-cluster(s) align with its main contribution.

    The four conditions

    Rate each from 0 (no workable route yet) to 4 (demonstrated fit). A 2 means feasible with a defined adjustment or support.

    I can sustain interest in this specific question through setbacks.

    I can do the required work with current skills, training, and supervision.Rate the available learning pathway, not innate intelligence or grades alone.

    The work can reach a defensible result within my available time.

    Equipment, materials, funding, and access have a workable path.Include shared resources, collaborators, and frugal alternatives.

    Research quality

    Rate each from 0 to 4 using the same anchors.

    The knowledge or result has a clear reason to matter.

    I can identify a specific unanswered question or useful gap.

    I can describe measurements, comparisons, or evidence that will answer it.

    The required adviser, collaborators, or technical support are accessible.

    Sub-cluster alignment

    For each direction, rate your interest and how central its contribution would be to this project. A project may fit more than one.

    ARTIFICER · I want to develop or combine tools for chemistry.

    ARTIFICER · A chemistry tool or interdisciplinary instrument is the main contribution.Examples: multispectral or gas sensors, DIY goniometers, splash measurement.

    PRIME · I want to investigate polymers or fibers.

    PRIME · Polymer or fiber chemistry, properties, or processing are the main contribution.Synthetic, biobased, and natural polymer work all belong here.

    KAMPILAN · I want to investigate materials and cultural heritage.

    KAMPILAN · Heritage chemistry and its context are the main contribution.Dyes and colors are current examples, not the limit of the sub-cluster.

    What resource mechanism is most realistic?

    Choose the pathway available to this project. This guides the recommendation; it does not add or remove points.

    Have safety, ethical, and facility requirements been considered for this idea?

    This is a discussion checkpoint, not part of the numeric score.

    Project assessment

    Project readiness / 100

    Possible sub-cluster alignment

    Alignment = 70% central contribution + 30% interest. Readiness = 15 points each for the four conditions and 10 points each for the four research quality checks. These are conversation aids, not a measure of intelligence or an admission decision.
